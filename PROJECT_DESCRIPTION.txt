My personal portfolio website introduces me as a second-year B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Bharath Institute of Higher Education and Research. Built using HTML5 and CSS3 only, it includes Home, About, Skills, Projects, Contact, and Footer sections. The dark navy design uses electric-blue accents, my photographs, responsive Grid and Flexbox layouts, and CSS-only animation with a reduced-motion alternative. Visitors can navigate between sections, view my completed portfolio and two clearly labelled planned projects, and contact me through email, GitHub, LinkedIn, or GUVI. The site includes keyboard focus styles, meaningful image descriptions, and layouts tested at desktop, tablet, and mobile widths.
